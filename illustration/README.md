# NICER illustration

`gen_illustration.py OUTDIR [text|none]` writes `nicer-illustration.svg` (no labels, final) or `nicer-illustration-labels.svg`. `build.sh` at the repository root also exports the PNGs (2008 px square, 600 dpi at 85 mm).

Shares the logo's tile shape (corner ratio 28/128), palette, corner sun, and the marine ice sheet with its floating shelf on a flat sea.
