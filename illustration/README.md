# NICER illustration

`gen_illustration.py OUTDIR [text|none]` writes `nicer-illustration.svg` (no labels) or `nicer-illustration-labels.svg`. `build.sh` at the repository root also exports the PNGs (2008 px square, 600 dpi at 85 mm).

`gen_round.py OUTDIR` writes `nicer-illustration-round.svg`, a vector redraw of the consortium's hand-drawn round badge without lettering.

The tile version shares the logo's tile shape (corner ratio 28/128), palette, corner sun, and the marine ice sheet with its floating shelf on a flat sea.
